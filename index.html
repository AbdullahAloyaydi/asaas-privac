Privacy Policy for Asaas App

Last Updated: March 27, 2026

1. Data Collection

Asaas is designed to respect your privacy. The app operates entirely offline and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, user accounts, or contact information to our servers.

2. Camera Usage (AR)

The app requires access to your device's camera solely for the Augmented Reality (AR) measurement feature. All AR processing and measurements are done locally on your device. We do not record, save, or upload any images or video feeds.

3. Subscriptions & Payments

We offer auto-renewable subscriptions to unlock premium features. All payment processing is securely handled by Apple directly (via App Store). We do not have access to your credit card information or Apple ID details.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

If you have any questions, please contact us via our official support channels.
